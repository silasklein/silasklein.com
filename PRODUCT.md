## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site, deployed to GitHub Pages with the owner's domain (registered at Locaweb). Chosen by the owner to leave Squarespace.

## Users

1. International peers and committees (biblical studies, history of religion, ancient Near Eastern iconography) evaluating him for invitations, cooperation and positions. They know the field well.
2. Event organizers and press looking for a bio, photo and topics.
3. Brazilian and Latin American readers and students arriving from the Portuguese side.

## Product Purpose

A personal showcase with one thesis and a way to get in touch. It is not an archive: Lattes, ORCID and Zotero already hold everything. Success is a visitor understanding the thesis within the first screen and writing to him.

## Positioning

Thesis: images mediate religion. He studies how seals, amulets and iconography of the ancient southern Levant made religion present, and how scholarly tools mediate what we see in them. The differentiator no peer can copy: two decades as an art director (Publicis, Coca-Cola, Netflix, Rio 2016) before academia. The eye that made images now reads ancient ones. Descriptor: "(Ancient) Religion\s, Media & Images".

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fully bilingual: Portuguese at the root, English under /en/. Portuguese opens first because Brazilian visitors find navigation harder than international ones. A third language may come later.
- Name: "Silas Klein" everywhere on the site; "Klein Cardoso" only where he is cited (publications, programmes, figure credits).
- Long one-page home with anchor navigation plus a few deep pages (current projects, design cases, news).
- No supervision section. No full publication list (link out to Zotero, ORCID, Lattes).
- Design is NOT offered as a service. Section 07 holds art direction (40 clients, a mosaic of 10 cases) and "Design for research" (four case pages at /design/<slug>: SSSL, Paleographic Networks, Observatório, EpOD).
- English title "Dean"; in Portuguese "Coordenador do PPG", never "Pró-Reitor".
- The hero (descriptor, subtitle, links) is settled: the owner chose on 2026-11-02 to keep it as it is.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: 19×11 module pixel monogram, #C761AE, always pixel-perfect (integer scales only, crisp edges, whole-module motion). Source: ../brand/logo.svg, original EPS in Dropbox "3. DA/313.eps".
- Colour (chosen 2026-10-31 from a specimen, replacing the old sage scheme): cool white #F5F6F4, near-black #15161D, the logo's magenta #C761AE as the only colour.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (owner's choice to pair with the pixel monogram).
- Vertical scroll only (owner rejected horizontal scroll). Impactful but not cluttered.
- Signature backslash "\s" (religion\s) and magenta "&".
- Owner wants a fluid site with parallax on text and images, and motion written in code.
- Owner rejected a "too tidy, aligned, AI-looking" layout. Binding references for text/image behaviour: his own Solidum site (dark, staggered display type, offset columns), Doubleleft site (mosaic grid of image tiles and text blocks), and a Bose editorial landing (vertical giant type, colour blocks overlapping photos, broken grid, layered images).
