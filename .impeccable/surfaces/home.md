# Surface brief: home (/, /pt/)

Mode: experience

## Direction contract (v2, 2026-10-31: owner rejected horizontal scroll, condensed type, and the noisy night layering)

THESIS: One vertical, high-contrast editorial page. Big Plus Jakarta Sans display type and asymmetric 12-column placement carry the impact. It refuses the centred, evenly stacked section column, and it refuses decoration noise.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white #F5F6F4 and alt #EBEDEA. Near-black #15161D for text and the dark bands (Design, Contact). The logo's magenta #C761AE is the ONLY colour, used for large fields (hero block, research parallelogram, contact square), "\" and "&". Plus Jakarta Sans 400–800. Parallelogram photos, numbered "01 ___" labels, 19×11 pixel monogram.

STORY: Descriptor and portrait, then what he does now, the research thesis and projects, books, teaching, initiatives, the design hand, a bio for organizers, and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Stacked "(Ancient) / Religion\s, / Media & / Images" on the left 7 columns. Parallelogram portrait on the right 5 columns, overlapped by a magenta square. Subtitle and two buttons below the title.

FORM: Vertical editorial with scroll-linked parallax (display lines, photos and colour fields at their own depth). Chosen by the owner from the specimen: type A, palette 3.
