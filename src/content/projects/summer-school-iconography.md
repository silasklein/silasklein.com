summary:
  en: "It teaches how to read ancient images as sources: methods of visual interpretation, ancient Near Eastern iconography and interartistic approaches to the Bible, with hands-on work on artifacts. In the tradition of the Fribourg School, visual culture is read alongside ancient literature, epigraphy and archaeology. Each edition has 20 contact hours (2 ECTS), taught in English. The 2024 theme was *Iconography of the Ancient Southern Levant*; since 2025, *Iconography of the Ancient World*."

Na tradição da Escola de Friburgo, a cultura visual é lida ao lado da literatura antiga, da epigrafia e da arqueologia. Cada edição tem 20 horas-aula (2 ECTS), em inglês. En la tradición de la Escuela de Friburgo, la cultura visual se lee junto con la literatura antigua, la epigrafía y la arqueología. Cada edición tiene 20 horas de clase (2 ECTS), en inglés. En 2024, el tema fue *Iconography of the Ancient Southern Levant*; desde 2025, *Iconography of the Ancient World*."
