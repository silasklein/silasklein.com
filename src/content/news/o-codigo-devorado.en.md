**O código devorado: os usos da iconografia antigo-oriental na exegese bíblica** KLEIN CARDOSO, Silas. **O código devorado: os usos da iconografia antigo-oriental na exegese bíblica**. São Paulo: Recriar, 2025.

My book *“O código devorado: os usos da iconografia antigo-oriental na exegese bíblica”* (The Devoured Code: The Uses of Ancient Near Eastern Iconography in Biblical Exegesis) was recently published by Editora Recriar. It translates into Brazilian Portuguese and expands upon my habilitation thesis (*Habilitationsschrift*), which was accepted at the Faculty of Theology of the University of Bern.

The book discusses interartistic approaches to the Bible, i.e., biblical-exegetical approaches that benefit from comparing biblical texts with visual remains supposedly originating from the same historical-cultural horizon. As its main contributions, the book offers the most comprehensive sociomaterial evaluation of the history of research, provides a theoretical and methodological reassessment of these different approaches, and proposes a new decolonial theory of interartistic interpretation based on the Brazilian Anthropophagic movement.

The book is available in print on the publisher's website ([see here](https://www.editorarecriar.com/catalogo-completo/o-codigo-devorado-silas-klein-cardoso)) and, thanks to funding from the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF), can be downloaded for free ([download book here](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15133151)).
