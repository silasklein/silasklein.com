Last week, from September 14–18, the III *International Summer School: Iconography of the Ancient World* took place at Faculdade Unida de Vitória, the school’s first edition in Latin America. The course is the result of a partnership between Faculdade Unida de Vitória and Bergische Universität Wuppertal, was organized by PD Dr Thomas Wagner and me, and is endorsed by ABIB and EABS.

The theme of this edition was *“Ancestral Visual Epistemologies”*. We began with introductions to the iconography of the ancient world and to visual epistemology, and went on to study images in rituals. We also welcomed two guest speakers who are leading figures in the field: Prof. Dr. Katia Pozzer (UFRGS), with the workshop “The Iconography of Mesopotamian Cylinder Seals”, and Prof. Dr. Thaís Rocha da Silva (UFMG), with the workshop “Iconography and Gender in Ancient Egypt”.

Katia Pozzer highlighted the multidisciplinary character of the school: “the event is very rich, very interesting, it has a multidisciplinary perspective. We have several fields brought together, specialists in the biblical, Egyptian and Mesopotamian worlds.” Thaís Rocha da Silva, in turn, valued “the opportunity to discuss with people from other fields, with fellow specialists, with students and, above all, with people we are not traditionally in the habit of talking to: people from religious studies and theology” (both quotes translated from Portuguese).

Over the week, we moved through Mesopotamia, Egypt, contemporary art and Indigenous traditions, with guided visits and fieldwork: an exhibition at a museum in Vitória and an Indigenous community. For Thomas Wagner, the visit to the Indigenous community had a special effect on the group, as it was there “where we really came in contact with people living in a different world order”. On the last day, participants presented and discussed their own research.

The videos of the week, made by Mauricio Bazilio, are on the Instagram account of the Observatório de Universos Bíblicos: a [summary of the school](https://www.instagram.com/universos.biblicos/reel/Ddru-jzOgV1/), with Thomas Wagner, and interviews with [Katia Pozzer](https://www.instagram.com/universos.biblicos/reel/DdwOnpgPqMJ/) and [Thaís Rocha da Silva](https://www.instagram.com/universos.biblicos/reel/Dd1bpNjurrR/) (in Portuguese).

In 2027, the summer school moves on to Prague.
