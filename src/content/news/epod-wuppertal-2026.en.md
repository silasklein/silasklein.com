Since October 2026, I have been an associate researcher in the international Research Unit *Epistemology of the Divine* (DFG FOR 5949), which brings together the universities of Bonn, Leipzig, Vienna and Wuppertal from 2026 to 2030. I work as a specialist in ancient iconography and visual mediation in sub-project 3, *Daily Visible: Towards an Epistemology of the Divine Mediated Through (the) Pictures*, based in Wuppertal and led by PD Dr. Thomas Wagner.

The sub-project studies stamp and cylinder seals from first-millennium BCE Palestine/Israel as mass media that conveyed knowledge about the divine, using digital methods such as AI-assisted image analysis and geospatial and epistemic network analysis. My work in the unit is part of the institutional research project *Borderline Divine Manifestations in the Biblical World*, which I coordinate at FUV.

Between November 2026 and February 2027, I will be at the Bergische Universität Wuppertal for an intensive research period, made possible by two grants: one from the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG; FOR 5949) and one from the Espírito Santo Research and Innovation Foundation (FAPES; call 04/2026). Besides research and joint publications, the programme for these three months includes lectures and conversations with universities, museums and funding agencies about new partnerships.
