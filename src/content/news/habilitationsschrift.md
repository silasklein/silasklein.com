**ENG:** I am happy, grateful, and humbled to have completed yesterday my habilitation procedure in Protestant Theology (Old Testament with a focus on ancient Israel and its neighboring cultures) at the Faculty of Theology of the University of Bern. After having my postdoctoral thesis (*Habilitationsschrift*) "*The Devoured Code: Southern Perspectives on Interartistic Approaches to the Bible*" accepted in May 2024, my habilitation lecture "Hermeneutics of the One: Temple/s, God/dess/es, and Religion\s in Iron Age Judah" was presented and approved yesterday, completing the process.

**PT-BR:** Estou muito feliz, grato e honrado em compartilhar que concluí ontem o meu processo de Livre-Docência (*Habilitation*) em Teologia Protestante (Antigo Testamento, com foco no antigo Israel e suas culturas vizinhas) na Faculdade de Teologia da Universidade de Berna (Universität Bern).

Após ter minha tese de pós-doutorado (*Habilitationsschrift*), intitulada "*The Devoured Code: Southern Perspectives on Interartistic Approaches to the Bible*", aprovada em maio de 2024, apresentei minha aula pública de habilitação, "*Hermeneutics of the One: Temple/s, God/dess/es, and Religion\s in Iron Age Judah*". A apresentação foi aprovada ontem, marcando a conclusão oficial de todo o processo.
