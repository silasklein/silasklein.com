**Challenging Dichotomies and Biases in the Study of the Ancient Southern Levant** BIERMANN, Bruno; KLEIN CARDOSO, Silas; PORZIA, Fabio; UEHLINGER, Christoph (Orgs.). **Challenging Dichotomies and Biases in the Study of the Ancient Southern Levant**. Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 2024. (Die Welt des Orients, Supplementary Issue). **ENG: **The book "Challenging Dichotomies and Biases in the Study of the Ancient Southern Levant," which I co-edited with Bruno Biermann, Fabio Porzia, and Christoph Uehlinger, was recently published by Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht.

As its title shows, the book addresses dichotomies and paradigms that have shaped or influenced the study of the region that comprises the modern states of Israel, Jordan, and Palestine from the second to the first millennium BCE. The work has contributions from specialists from different fields that, assuming different theoretical and methodological approaches, contribute from their perspective and datasets to the critical questioning of paradigms and approaches, especially those that shape historical reconstructions by simplifying complex, multifaceted, and fluid realities into clear-cut dichotomies.

Thanks to the generous support of the Swiss National Science Foundation SNSF, you can freely access, download, and read the book through this [link](https://www.vr-elibrary.de/doi/book/10.13109/9783666560972).

**PT:** O livro *"Challenging Dichotomies and Biases in the Study of the Ancient Southern Levant"* (Desafiando dicotomias e vieses no estudo do antigo Levante Meridional), que coeditei com Bruno Biermann, Fabio Porzia e Christoph Uehlinger, foi publicado recentemente pela editora Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht.

Como o título sugere, o livro aborda dicotomias e paradigmas que moldaram ou influenciaram o estudo da região que compreende os estados modernos de Israel, Jordânia e Palestina do segundo ao primeiro milênio a.E.C. A obra conta com contribuições de especialistas de diversas áreas que, adotando diferentes abordagens teóricas e metodológicas, contribuem com suas perspectivas e dados para o questionamento crítico de paradigmas estabelecidos — especialmente aqueles que moldam reconstruções históricas ao simplificar realidades complexas, multifacetadas e fluidas em dicotomias rígidas.
